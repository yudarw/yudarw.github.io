# Overview

5G New Radio (NR) aims to deliver ultra-fast mobile broadband (up to 20 Gbps), requiring wide, contiguous bandwidths. Due to congestion below 6 GHz, focus has shifted to millimeter wave (mm-wave) frequencies in the 24.25–52.6 GHz range (FR2). These high frequencies face severe path loss (over 60 dB/m) and are easily blocked by obstacles like humans or vehicles.To overcome these issues, beamforming with active antenna arrays (AAS) is used, integrating transceivers directly with antennas (no RF ports or cables). User Equipment (UE) also adopts compact solutions like Antenna on Chip (AoC) and Antenna in Package (AiP). Testing now relies heavily on Over-the-Air (OTA) methods, especially for FR2, requiring precise measurement environments.
