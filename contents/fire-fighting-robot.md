# Overview

Participated in the National Indonesia Fire Fighting Robot Contest 2017 at Brawijaya University, Indonesia. The mission is to estinguish the fire that is arbitrarily placed in room. The robot need to navigate through the rooms and coridors to find the fire and estinguish it. In this competition, our team achieve the best 5 finalist of 101 teams.


# Contributions
- Designed 3D model of the robot by using SOLIDWORK
- Designed electrical system and PCB
- Test the program of sensors, actuators, and the microcontroller
- Designed the inverse kinematic algorithm for 6 legged robot
- Contribute to the programming of the main robot controller
