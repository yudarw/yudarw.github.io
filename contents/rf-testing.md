# Overview

RF testing play an important role in our testing since most of our products are required the latest RF communication interfaces, i.e., WIFI 5, WIFI 6, WIFI 6E, NR5G, 5G FR2, and more. All of the RF parameter testing should be accurately measured to ensure the signal quality in both receiver and transmitter meet the customer requirements.

![RF Measurements](../docs/images/rf-measurements.JPG)

I was responsible for handling several tasks fro the RF testing, including:
- Setup environment, ensuring all of the SMA cables, antennas, and connectors are well connected
- Pathloss calibration
- Develop program for RF testing automation
- Design the OTA station for NR5G testing.

I also experienced hanlding DUT communication of several chipsets such as Qualcomm, Broadcom, Mediatek, and Queltec. Understanding the commands and protocols needed to setup the WIFI testing.
